1. Nuclear Interactions: We study the heavy ion interaction to know about the interaction mechanism. We hope to uncover the hidden laws of the universe by studying the nucleus. We wish to enrich  proper understanding of heavy ion interaction for proper use of these ions in cancer therapy.
2. Radiation Shielding and measurement: We deal with the interaction of radiation with matter and investigate the harmful effects of the secondary particles after fragmentation . Our research has real world applications in designing and developing the shielding material for space-shuttles to protect astronauts from radiation. This study can also help to assess the safety of astronauts from space radiation. 
3. Quantum Physics: We study the laws of physics from the quantum perspective which is essential to understand the universe at a micro level. Additionally, we wish to enhance Quantum Physics Education in our country.
4. Quantum Computing: We are preparing to take the quantum leap with the rest of the world. Quantum computing is one of the pioneering fields of research. The United Nations has designated 2025 as the International Year of Quantum Science and Technology (IYQ). We wish to develop efficient quantum state & energy teleportation protocols for quantum communication.
