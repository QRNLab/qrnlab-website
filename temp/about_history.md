Motto: Bridging Nuclear, Quantum, and Astrophysical Frontiers through Research and Innovation

About:
QRNLab is a multidisciplinary research group dedicated to advancing fundamental and applied physics.

Our work spans a wide spectrum of domains, from nuclear and medical physics to quantum computing and astrophysics. The lab operates under the guidance of Dr. Quazi Muhammad Rashed Nizam, whose expertise in nuclear physics forms the foundation of our experimental and theoretical investigations.

Our aim is to foster collaboration among students and researchers passionate about exploring the intersection of classical and quantum domains, driving innovation across physics disciplines.

Motivation:
1. To find out the hidden laws of the universe by investigating the nucleus
2. To offer a helping hand to students.
