Md. Ashik Azad Khan Anik is a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Northern University Bangladesh. He completed his M.Sc. in Physics at the University of Chittagong under the supervision of Prof. Quazi Muhammad Rashed Nizam, where his research focused on environmental radiation monitoring and mapping of naturally occurring radioactive materials (NORM) in Bangladesh.
