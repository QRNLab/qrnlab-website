Welcome to the website of QRNLab, a multidisciplinary research group at the Department of Physics, University of Chittagong. This space will host lab news, notes, and updates on our ongoing research.

Our work spans four broad domains:

1. Nuclear interactions of heavy ions
2. Radiation shielding and measurement
3. Quantum physics
4. Quantum computing

We believe research should be a two-way street: we push the boundaries of physics while offering a helping hand to students interested in these topics. If you are a student at the University of Chittagong who wants to get involved, reach out — the lab welcomes motivated newcomers.

Stay tuned for more posts, including lecture notes and commentary on our published work.
